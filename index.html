Pause privacy policy

App: Pause: Mindful Scroll Blocker (com.sreeharim.pause) · Developer: Sreehari M · Updated 1 November 2026

In short: Pause does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any of your data. It has no internet access. Everything it keeps stays on your phone.

What Pause does

Pause shows a short countdown before an app you've chosen (for example a social media app) opens, so you can decide whether you really want to open it.

Permissions

Pause asks for two Android permissions, and uses them only for its countdown:

  • Usage access: to know which app is in front, so Pause can tell when you open an app you chose. Pause reads only the package name of that app (for example com.instagram.android), never the content of any app, your messages or anything you type. It does not keep a history of the apps you use.
  • Display over other apps: to show the countdown on top of the app you opened.

While it is on, Pause runs a small background service, shown by Android as a "Pause is on" notification. You are shown what each permission is for before you grant it, and you can turn either one off at any time in Android's Settings.

Earlier versions (1.0.x) used Android's Accessibility service for the same purpose, reading only the name of the app on screen. Version 1.1.0 and later no longer use it.

Data stored on your phone

Pause saves the following only in its private storage on your device:

  • The list of apps you chose to pause.
  • Your settings (countdown length, grace period, appearance) and any messages you write.
  • Simple daily counts: how many times each chosen app was paused, skipped or opened anyway. Counts older than 35 days are deleted automatically.

To see the list of your apps, Pause reads the names and icons of apps installed on your phone. This list is only shown to you and is never stored or shared.

This data is not included in Android's cloud backup. Uninstalling Pause, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of it.

What Pause does not do

  • No internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.
  • No accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Children

Pause is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
